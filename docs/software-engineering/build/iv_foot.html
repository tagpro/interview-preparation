What this page is not. It is a map, not a textbook. Eight of the areas are worked through properly on the other nine pages in this series — foundations, Go, cloud, Python, Java, AWS, algorithms and AI engineering, all linked in the rail. The point of a map is to show you the ground you have not walked.
