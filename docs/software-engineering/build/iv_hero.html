Not a question bank. A map of the subject — thirteen areas, forty-six topics, and under each one the full list of things that can come up, with a line on what the question is really measuring. The point is coverage: you should be able to read this page and find the words you do not know yet. Where a topic has a page of its own elsewhere in this series, it says so and links there rather than repeating it.

5–7 roundsA typical senior loopA screen, one or two coding rounds, system design, a low-level design or deep dive, and a hiring-manager conversation. Every one is written up against the same rubric.
~35 minOf a 45-minute roundIntros and your questions take the rest. A perfect answer at minute 44 scores below a good one explained at minute 30, because the interviewer has to write down what they saw.
4 signalsWhat is actually scoredProblem solving, technical depth, code or design quality, and communication. Everything below is a way of producing evidence for one of those four.
